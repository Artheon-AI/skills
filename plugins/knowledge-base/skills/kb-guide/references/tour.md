# Guided tour

The tour shows the person their own base: you fetch each stop with the Notion tools, say what it is in a sentence or two, and give its Notion link so they open it alongside you. You only read. Skip stops the person cannot open or their copy does not have.

## Finding each stop

- Pages: the links in the two Agent Instructions pages first; otherwise `notion-search` by the exact title.
- Database views: fetch the database from the databases table in Agent Instructions; the result lists its views with their URLs. `notion-query-data-sources` in view mode shows a view's rows when a few examples help.
- Give the person the page or view URL to click. Show three to five example rows at most, by title.

## Route

1. **Home - <Company>.** Walk the Home view top to bottom: Start here, Knowledge hubs, Modules, Databases, At a glance. Point out that the tabs are live views, not copies.
2. **One hub.** Pick the hub that fits the person's role (Customers and offer for sales, How we work for delivery, Company for an owner). Show two or three of its pages, their Status, and its Records tabs.
3. **Databases.** Open the module page the person will use most (Project management for delivery, CRM for sales) and its tabs. Then open one database from it. Switch between two of its named views and show how a filter changes what you see, never the data. Open one record: pinned properties at the top, relation tabs, template headings.
4. **Their personal space.** Home - <Name>: the New capture button and the link to today's daily note side by side at the top, then Start here, Areas (with Automations, their own scheduled jobs, in the AI area) and the My records tabs. Open today's daily note and show "Notes that day" at its end. Then the Databases page and the Notes Captures view. Mention Open on start, their own setting ([structure.md](structure.md), "Personal space").
5. **Agent Instructions.** Show "Where things go" and "Ask first" on the company page, and "About me" on their own page. Suggest they keep "Now" current, since their agent reads it every conversation.

Check in after each stop; the person may want to linger on one or skip the rest.

## Sample tasks

Offer two or three, matched to the person. They do them in Notion while you watch through the tools, or you do them after a clear yes.

- **Make a capture.** Press New capture, write one line, then find it in the Captures view. Tomorrow the nightly job will have filed it and linked it to today's daily note, where it shows under "Notes that day".
- **Find a client.** Ask "What do we know about <a client from the Clients view>?" or find it yourself by search and by the Clients view, then follow its relations to Projects and Meetings.
- **Read a hub.** Open the hub for their work and read one Current page end to end; note who owns it and when it was last edited.
- **Add a to-do.** Add one line under Work in today's daily note.
- **Create a record from a template.** Add a Meetings row for a real recent meeting with the Meeting template. Offer to fill it from their notes after a yes.
- **See an approval stop.** Ask an agent to change a page ticked Requires edit approval, such as Offer and pricing, and watch it propose the change and wait.
- **See what changed.** Open "Edited recently" on the company wiki and one database.

After the tasks, give three or four example requests for their agent, for example "note that ...", "what did we decide about ...", "prepare me for the meeting with ...", "where should this go?".
